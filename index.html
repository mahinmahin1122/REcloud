Questions

Is REcloud free?

Yes. You get free storage when you sign up, and you can upgrade any time if you need more space.

How do I install the Android app?

Download the APK, open it, and allow installs from your browser if your phone asks. Then sign in with your account.

Can I use one account on several devices?

Yes. Sign in on as many phones and computers as you like. Everything stays in sync.

What if I lose my phone?

Sign in from another device and remove the lost phone from your account. Your files remain safe in REcloud.
